## 2. What was built

A dedicated page at **`/work`**, not a homepage section. The homepage is seven
`position:fixed` stages driven by a scroll timeline — adding an eighth means
retuning the stage windows, the palette stops, the nav, and the analytics
`STAGE_AT` map. `CLAUDE.md` warns against exactly that, and a gallery is not
worth the risk to a working engine.

- **Hero** — dark green, Bebas display type, the existing token set. Three real
  counts (29 pieces / 4 brands / 7 sites), no invented ones.
- **Filters** — All work · Campaign creative (12) · Product photography (10) ·
  Web development (7). Only categories holding real assets are rendered. Sticky,
  with a live count on each, and the chosen filter goes into the URL
  (`?filter=web`) with `replaceState` so a view can be shared without stuffing
  the back button.
- **Gallery** — 29 tiles on a 6-column grid with art-directed spans: featured
  pieces at half-width, banners full-width, supporting work smaller. Each tile
  reserves its box from the **real pixel dimensions**, so nothing shifts while
  images arrive.
- **Brand blocks** — LARO, IRIS, Arlux and the client storefronts, each with
  what it is, what was delivered, and a link to the live site. **Arlux links to
  the media-buying case study** rather than repeating its figures, so the two
  pages can never disagree.
- **CTA** — "Your brand could be our next case study" → `index.html#book`,
  which opens the existing booking modal. No new form was created.
- **Lightbox** — full-size view, prev/next within the *filtered* set, Escape,
  arrow keys, focus trap, focus returned to the tile, scroll lock, swipe on
  touch, and the `src` cleared on close so nothing keeps downloading.

### No invented content

No fabricated client names, results, testimonials, objectives or metrics. Every
caption states what the piece is; every brand blurb describes what the work
shows. The only numbers on the page are the three counts, which are computed
from the data file.
